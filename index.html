    WELCOME TO PUMPERS CITY

    Pumpers City is a pixel city you explore right in your browser. Treasure boxes are hidden across seven districts. Every box gives you XP, and some of them hold real $PUMP.

    Getting started

    1. Play as guest. No wallet needed. You get a basic character and can explore every district, but you can't open boxes.
    2. Connect your wallet (Phantom, Solflare or Backpack). We check your $PUMPERS tokens and Pumpers NFTs and set your level. If you own NFTs, you pick one and play as that character.
    3. Find a box, stand next to it, pick the lock. Collect XP, climb the daily leaderboard and hope for a prize.

    We never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Connecting only shares your public address. Signing the login message is free and moves no funds.

    BOXES & PRIZES

    Boxes glow gold and are spread across every district. To open one:

    1. Stand still next to the box. You can't open boxes while walking past.
    2. Pick the lock. A needle slides along a bar. Press E, Space or OPEN while the needle is inside the gold zone.
    3. If you miss, the lock jams for 2 seconds, then you can try again.

    XP IN EVERY BOX

    Every box gives XP, between 5 and 25 before your multiplier. Higher rolls are rarer.

    REAL $PUMP

    Some boxes hold a $PUMP prize. You only find out when you open one. Claim it from MY PRIZES and it goes straight to your wallet.

    RELEASED IN BATCHES

    Prize boxes go live a few at a time. When a batch is found, the next one goes live somewhere in the city.

    EVERYONE SEES IT

    When someone opens a prize, every player sees it on screen.

    Which box holds a prize is decided by our server when the box is opened. Nobody, including your browser, can see it in advance.

    How rarity is decided

    Every trait has a rarity score: the fewer Pumpers share it, the higher the score. A Pumper's tier comes from the total score of all five traits, not from any single trait. A crown alone won't make a Legendary, but a crown with a gold skin will.

    FAIR PLAY

    • Movement and box opening are checked by our server. Teleporting, speed hacks and auto-openers don't work.
    • Each wallet has limits on how many boxes it can open, so bots and multi-wallets gain nothing.
    • Holdings are checked when you connect and again when you claim a prize.
    • Suspicious wins can be held for review before payout.

    FAQ

    Do I need a wallet to play?

    No. Guests can explore every district. You need a wallet with tokens or NFTs to open boxes.

    Which wallets work?

    Phantom, Solflare and Backpack. On a phone, open this site inside your wallet app's browser.

    Is it safe to connect?

    Connecting only shares your public address. The login signature is free and can't move funds. We will never ask for your seed phrase. Anyone who does is a scammer.

    I bought tokens. Why can't I open boxes yet?

    Reconnect your wallet so your holdings are checked again, and make sure the tokens are in the wallet you connected.

    I own several Pumpers. Which one do I play as?

    You choose. After connecting, pick any Pumper you own, and switch any time in the game with SWITCH PUMPER. Your level and perks always come from your best one.

    Does it work on phones?

    Yes. Hold or drag to walk, tap a box to open it, and tap the minimap for the big map.

    How do I claim my prize?

    Open MY PRIZES at the top of the screen while playing and press CLAIM. You sign a free message in your wallet, we check your holdings again, and the $PUMP is sent to the wallet you played with within seconds. Unclaimed prizes wait for you, so you can claim them any time.
